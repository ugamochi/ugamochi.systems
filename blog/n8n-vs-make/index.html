n8n vs Make.com (2026): Which Automation Tool Is Right for Your Business?

A hands-on breakdown of the two leading workflow automation platforms — covering pricing, self-hosting, integrations, and the exact scenarios where each one wins.

By Pavel Ugamoti March 2026 12 min read

If you've been searching for the right workflow automation tool in 2026, you've almost certainly landed on n8n and Make.com as your top two candidates. They're the most capable platforms at their price point, and they're genuinely different in ways that matter.

I've deployed both tools across dozens of client engagements. Here's my honest breakdown of where each one wins, where it falls short, and how to decide which is right for your situation.

Bottom line up front: Choose n8n if you want self-hosting, unlimited executions, and full code access. Choose Make.com if you want the most beginner-friendly visual editor and don't want to manage infrastructure.

Pricing: n8n vs Make.com

Self-hosted n8n is free. You install it on your own server and pay only for hosting — typically €5–20/month. There are no execution limits, no per-operation charges, and no seat caps on the community edition.

Make.com charges per operation — each time a module (step) in your workflow runs, it consumes one operation. A 5-step workflow triggered 1,000 times costs 5,000 operations. The costs escalate fast for high-volume users.

Ease of Use

Make.com: beginner-friendly

Make.com's visual editor is genuinely excellent. Non-technical team members can build functional Make.com automations with a few hours of practice.

n8n: powerful but technical

n8n's interface is node-based. It requires more comfort with concepts like webhooks, HTTP requests, and JSON. Once it clicks, it's faster to build complex branching logic and sub-workflows.

Self-Hosting & Data Sovereignty

This is a critical consideration for law firms and accounting practices.

With Make.com, your data passes through their servers. With n8n self-hosted, your data never leaves your infrastructure. This is why I exclusively recommend n8n for industries where data sovereignty is non-negotiable.

AI & Agentic Automation in 2026

n8n has leaned into AI agent nodes hard. You can build a full AI agent workflow entirely within n8n: the agent receives input, queries a knowledge base, decides what action to take, and executes it autonomously.

Conclusion

Make.com wins on approachability and native integration breadth. n8n wins on cost, data control, AI capabilities, and raw power.

For the professional services businesses I work with — law firms, accounting practices, marketing agencies — n8n is almost always the right answer.

Frequently Asked Questions
